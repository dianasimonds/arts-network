Diana Simonds

MA student in Digital Art History and Computational Media at Duke University

Diana Simonds is a current MA student at Duke University studying Digital Art History and Computational Media. With a professional background in the museum space, she returned to school to explore questions around networking in the arts space and to imagine more connected ways for artists and institutions to find one another.

Digital Art History Computational Media Arts Networking

About

Diana's work sits at the intersection of art, technology, and cultural infrastructure. Drawing on her museum background and graduate research, she is interested in how digital tools can support discovery, visibility, and stronger relationships across the arts ecosystem.

Research Focus

  • Digital art history and networked cultural research
  • Computational approaches to mapping arts communities
  • Designing tools that make the arts space more legible and connected

Details

  • Location: Durham, NC, USA
  • Affiliation: Duke University
  • Field: Digital Art History and Computational Media
  • Project: CATENARTS arts network map

Project Statement

"This project began as a way to look for answers to questions about networking in the arts space. CATENARTS serves as one potential response: a platform for making relationships, proximity, and artistic communities more visible."
