Maya Ellison

Interdisciplinary artist based in Durham, North Carolina

Maya Ellison creates installations, print works, and community-based projects that examine memory, architecture, and regional storytelling. This page is a sample layout you can reuse later for real artist profiles.

Installation Printmaking Community Practice

About

Ellison's practice draws from oral histories, site visits, and archival fragments. Her recent work layers handmade paper, projected light, and sculptural forms to explore how neighborhoods remember change.

Recent Exhibitions

  • 2026, Folded Ground, Durham Project Space
  • 2025, River Room, Triangle Contemporary
  • 2024, Soft Structures, Common Thread Gallery

Details

  • Location: Durham, NC, USA
  • Affiliation: Independent artist and educator
  • Website: mayaellisonstudio.com
  • Email: hello@mayaellisonstudio.com

Artist Statement

"I’m interested in the emotional afterlife of built spaces. My work turns collected traces into temporary environments where viewers can move through layered histories rather than simply observe them."
